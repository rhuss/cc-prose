Kubernetes provides a declarative configuration model for managing containerized applications. The platform serves as a robust foundation for orchestrating workloads across clusters, leveraging its built-in scheduling capabilities to ensure high availability.

Additionally, the ecosystem has garnered significant attention from enterprise adopters. Industry reports highlight the crucial role that container orchestration plays in the evolving landscape of cloud-native development, underscoring the importance of standardized deployment patterns.

The control plane features a distributed key-value store (etcd) that maintains cluster state. It boasts automatic failover and leader election, ensuring that the system remains operational during node failures.
